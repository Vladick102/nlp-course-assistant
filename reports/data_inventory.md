Generated by `scripts/inventory.py` from `data/parsed/documents.json`, `data/parsed/ingest_stats.json` and the raw PDFs (PyMuPDF). Raw files in `pok/`, `aks/`, `os/` are never modified; `.pptx` files are converted to PDF by LibreOffice (`data/converted/`). Hand review of every deck is in `reports/review/{pok,aks,os}.md` (written by reading sampled pages, not the full text).

## 1. Corpus at a glance

- **64 indexed documents** from 69 raw files (after de-duplication and 3 explicit exclusions); types: lab 5, lecture 54, logistics 4, other 1.
- Documents per course (a shared deck counts for each of its courses): АКС 18, ОС 24, ПОК 29.
- **3447 PDF pages** → **3337 slides** after overlay merge (4 merged) and dropping empty pages (106).
- 1071 slides flagged `needs_vision` (figures/diagrams to describe with the VLM); 427 slides without a title (genuinely untitled Beamer frames); 896 slides with external links (2575 link annotations in the PDFs).
- Raw-text defects measured *before* normalization: 86637 characters of cp1251 mojibake in 9 documents (357 pages) and 12591 Latin `i`/`I` characters used inside Ukrainian words (in 29 documents).

## 3. Quirks and decisions

Each row is a finding from data understanding (`reports/review/*.md`, plus measurements above), the decision it
led to, and where the decision lives. "Reviewed" means sampled pages were read by an agent; counts such as
"all 51 pages" come from that review or from the numbers in the table.

| # | Finding | Evidence | Decision / implementation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Beamer decks repeat a section-navigation bar (top) and a footer `Олег Фаренюк <title>` on every page | all Beamer decks and the requirements deck | Lines repeated on > `ingest.repeat_ratio` of pages inside the header/footer bands are boilerplate (`parser.boilerplate_set`); the *active* nav entry (different colour) is kept as `Slide.section` metadata |
| 2 | Page numbers appear as lone digit lines (PowerPoint exports, Beamer) | Lek_*_2018, Beamer footers | Lines that normalise to digits/slashes only are dropped |
| 3 | cp1251 text decoded as Latin-1 (Type 3 fonts without ToUnicode): `Ìîòèâàöiÿ` for `Мотивація` | `os_l040_Linux_internals` (all pages), `pok_l01_physics_basics`, `pok_l02_demoboard`, `pok_l03_bool`, `pok_l04_simple_logic`, `pok_l05_sequential_logic`, `cpp1_app_binutils_n_gcc`, the pok copy of `pok_aks_os_l00_intro`; see `moj.` column | `textnorm.fix_mojibake` repairs a text line only when >= 30% of its letters are in `À-ÿ` (avoids touching French/German text), `¨` → `ї`; the registry also prefers the copy with less mojibake when the same file exists in two folders |
| 3a | The T2A dash glyph (0x15) is extracted as a control character | `os_l040_Linux_internals` p.2 («трохи – стандартною») | A lone control char between spaces becomes `–`; other control chars stay `"` (T2A quote glyphs) |
| 4 | Latin `i`/`I` used instead of Ukrainian `і`/`І` inside Cyrillic words | `cc_l25_simd`, `os_l0XX_GUI_*`, `lapp_qc`, requirements deck; see `lat-i` column | `textnorm.fix_latin_i` (iterated, also the conjunction «і» between Cyrillic words). English words and `i` in code are untouched. Without it BM25 would miss the same word typed with a Cyrillic `і` in a question |
| 5 | Footnote markers glued to words (`балів25`, `живучіший¹`) — dangerous for grading numbers | requirements p.21-27, `os_l025_signals` | Superscript digit spans are dropped when smaller than 0.8 of the line font; footnote text is captured separately in `Slide.footnotes` |
| 5a | Footnote text may sit *inside* the footer band, in a font slightly larger than the footer | `os_l000_intro` p.4 («Legacy, але все ще часто незамінні.») | Found while writing parser tests: small non-repeating text larger than the document's footer font is a footnote, not footer |
| 6 | Beamer bullets are tiny **images** (balls) or glyphs (`◇`, `◇◇`) | `os_l000_intro` p.4 | Found while writing parser tests: bullet images come from `page.get_image_info()` (explicit text flags hide image blocks), glyph runs give nesting depth, so lists are rendered as nested Markdown |
| 7 | Incremental builds (`\pause`) produce near-identical consecutive pages | `cc_l00` p.48-50, `os_l033_async` p.45-51, `os_l0XX_FS` p.36-41 | Overlay merge on title + text containment (`parser.merge_overlays`), keeping the fullest page and `page_start..page_end`; near-identical but *different* slides (real edits) are kept |
| 8 | Blank / nav-only / picture-only pages | `cc_l00` p.53, `Lec_1_OS` p.65, `pok_l04` p.113-114 | Empty after boilerplate removal and no figure → dropped (`dropped_empty`); picture-only pages stay if flagged for vision |
| 9 | Diagrams, circuits and charts have only scattered text labels or none | `pok_l04` p.40, `Lek_6_7` p.118, `proc_switching` | `figures.flag_slides` (large raster image, extra vector drawings vs. the document median, low text + graphics) → VLM description (`app/ingest/vlm.py`, prompt `vlm_figure`), always labelled as model-generated |
| 10 | External links: Beamer link annotations, sometimes wrapped over several lines; internal `goto` links are navigation | `os_l000_intro` p.10-12, 1948 distinct (page, URI) pairs over the corpus | Only http/ftp/mailto URIs, one entry per URI per slide, anchor text joined across wrapped rects; bare URLs shown as themselves; links are never injected inside code fences. Automatic check: `eval/parse_eval.py` (gold-free link recall over all documents) |
| 11 | Code listings: LuaTeX decks set code in Times, not a monospace font | `aks` decks (`cc_l50` p.34, `cc_l60` p.39) | **Accepted limitation**: such code is not fenced (no reliable signal without a language model); the text is still searchable. Monospace fonts (pdfTeX/Beamer decks) are fenced |
| 12 | Large-font body text in PowerPoint decks looks like headings | `Lek_3_2018` p.1-3 | A page where more than half of the kept lines are "heading sized" has no headings; numbered items (`4.`) start new lines |
| 13 | Table-of-contents pages with dot leaders in labs | all `cc_lab*` | Lines matching `. . . . 12` dropped (`textnorm.is_toc_line`) |
| 14 | Untitled Beamer frames exist | see `untitled` column | Genuine; the title falls back to the previous heading + «(продовження)» only for document-style pages |
| 15 | Two grading schemes for the same course (syllabus 2025 vs. requirements 2024/2025 vs. `Lec_1_OS` p.10) | `reports/review/os.md` #8, `pok.md` #11 | Logistics answers come only from the three syllabi and the 2024/2025 requirements deck (`doc_type = logistics`); the 2020/2021 requirements deck is excluded; `Lec_1_OS` is a lecture and never feeds logistics. ## 4. Known limitations and not checked

- Code in LuaTeX (aks) decks is not fenced; formulas break into text fragments (`cc_l40` p.3).
- Text inside raster images is only available through the VLM descriptions; their quality is evaluated in
  `eval/figure_eval.py` on `figure_gold`.
- Review findings come from sampled pages. Per-page correctness is measured instead by `eval/parse_eval.py`
  against `parse_gold` (40 slides) and by the automatic link-recall check.
- Whether the `.pptx` decks carry speaker notes was not checked (the converted PDFs have none).
